# Hearth & Tails Audio Asset License And Provenance

This folder contains project-specific music assets generated for this project.

## Scope

- Covered assets: `bgm_*`, `sting_*`, `amb_*`, `voc_*`, `sfx_*`, and `ui_*`
  files under `assets/audio/bgm/`, `assets/audio/sting/`,
  `assets/audio/ambient/`, `assets/audio/voc/`, `assets/audio/sfx/`, and
  `assets/audio/ui/`.
- Current generation batch: see `assets/audio/provenance/music_provenance_manifest.json`.
- Interaction/UI batch: see `assets/audio/provenance/sfx_provenance_manifest.json`.
- Ambience/visitor voice batch: see
  `assets/audio/provenance/ambient_voc_provenance_manifest.json`.
- Runtime `.m4a` files are platform-compatible transcodes of the declared WAV masters and inherit the same provenance.
- The distribution license is the repository root `ASSET_LICENSE`; the root
  Apache License 2.0 applies to source code, not these recordings.
- The exact bundled inventory and hashes are recorded in
  `assets/provenance/release_asset_manifest.json`.

## Source Declaration

The current batch was generated through procedural original synthesis from the Hearth & Tails audio specification:

- No third-party samples, sample packs, loops, or reference audio were used.
- No artist-name, composer-name, band-name, soundtrack-name, or commercial song prompts were used.
- No commercial melody, recording, or copyrighted reference track was intentionally reproduced.
- The generator source is `assets/audio/provenance/generate_music_assets.py`.
- Interaction and paper UI sounds are generated by
  `assets/audio/provenance/generate_sfx_assets.py`.
- Seamless yard ambience and visitor voice cues are generated by
  `assets/audio/provenance/generate_ambient_assets.py`.
- The 2026-08-02 `bgm_shop` revision is an original procedural variation of
  the project's own yard harmony and motif. Its felt-piano, nylon-guitar, muted
  wooden accent, and air layers contain no external sample or reference audio;
  `generate_music_assets.py --only shop` reproduces the source masters,
  runtime M4A, hashes, and loudness records without touching other music.
