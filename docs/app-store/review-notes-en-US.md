# App Review Notes (English, U.S.)

Petopia is a single-player, local-first companion game with on-device saves.

## Review Notes

- No sign-in, account, or demo account is required.
- The app has no ads or subscriptions. It offers three repeatable voluntary
  support products and one permanent, one-time support product.
- In-app purchases provide decorative thank-you gifts only. They do not change
  pet growth, game currency, interaction cooldowns, rarity odds, or access to
  gameplay content.
- There is no user-generated content, chat, or online social functionality.
- Pets, progress, compendiums, and postcards are stored on the device.
- Notifications are off by default. The system permission is requested only
  after the player enables a category in Settings > Gentle Reminders.
- Notifications are limited to in-game postcards, returning companions, and
  anniversaries. The app does not send generic engagement reminders.
- Settings > Saves & Privacy can export a save through the system share sheet
  and import only a file explicitly selected by the player.
- `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is `false`; the app implements no proprietary
  encryption.

## Suggested Review Path

1. Open the three-page welcome flow.
2. Adopt any available species and give the pet a name.
3. Use Feed, Pat, Toy, and Bath at the bottom of the garden.
4. Open the pet card to view Pet Details and the Growth Journal.
5. Open the journal menu and choose Album. A new save has no delivered letters,
   so select Preview a Travel Postcard to inspect the complete reading view.
   This preview does not alter progress.
6. Use the journal menu to inspect the compendiums, Shop, Visitor Compendium, and
   Settings.
7. In Settings > Garden Light > Support the Garden, inspect all four IAPs.
   Restore "Garden Keeper" is available at the bottom. The three ordinary
   support products are consumables and cannot be restored.
8. Settings also contains separate music, sound, and gentle-haptics controls,
   notification categories, privacy and help pages, and save import/export.

Apart from App Store purchases, purchase restoration, and webpages explicitly
opened by the player, travel, postcards, returning companions, and daily
visitors are driven entirely by local time and the on-device save.
